Set up the gait sensors

Do this once for each of the three M5StickS3 sticks. You need a Windows or Mac computer with Chrome or Edge, and a USB-C cable that carries data (not a charge-only cable).

1. Put the software on a stick

  1. Plug the stick into the computer with the USB-C cable.
  2. Click Install below. Pick the stick from the list (it's usually called USB JTAG/serial debug unit) and click Connect, then Install.
  3. If the stick isn't in the list: hold the power button on its side for about 2 seconds until a green light flashes inside, then click Install again.
  4. Wait about a minute. When it's done the stick restarts and shows HEAD on a blue screen.
This browser can't do this. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs to be opened over https.

2. Choose what each stick is

Press the front button to change it. The stick restarts and shows the new name.

HEAD
top of the head
PELVIS
top of the croup
R FORE
right front pastern

Make one of each, and put a strip of coloured tape on each stick so you can tell them apart.

  • Front button: change HEAD / PELVIS / R FORE (not while recording).
  • Hold the front button 2 seconds: turn off. To turn on, click the power button on the side once (double-click it also turns off).
  • The other small button: dim or brighten the screen.
  • Charge with any USB-C phone charger. A full charge lasts a morning of checks.

3. Using them

On the iPhone, install the free Bluefy web browser from the App Store and open the diary in Bluefy (Safari can't talk to Bluetooth gadgets). Then go to Gait → With the sensors. The diary shows where to fit each stick.
